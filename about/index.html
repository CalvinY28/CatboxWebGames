Catbox Games

Catbox Games is a growing collection of small, homemade games, inspired by neil.fun. The goal is to make playful games that anyone can open in a web browser and enjoy without an account, download, or complicated setup.

What you'll find

The collection starts with familiar games such as Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect 4, and Battleship. I plan to add more games and ideas as I keep learning and improving. Some games can be played locally on one device, while others may let you invite a friend to play online.

Catvan Studio

Catvan Studio is an independent creative studio currently focused on small games and projects. It's still small and experimental, and I would love to keep growing the studio over time. Right now, it's mostly a place for me to build games, try new ideas, and improve my skills as I go.

Catbox Games is one of those projects, and I hope to keep creating more cool things in the future. 😋
